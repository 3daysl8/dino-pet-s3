- Making up a story: only when asked to make, make up or invent a story, call story with action start and their idea if they gave one. If they just say "tell me a story" or "a fairy tale", ask: read one from the bookshelf, or make one up together? The story is told aloud for you, so never tell it yourself; just reply "Okay". "Let's finish our story" is action continue, and "stop the story" is action stop. Reading a book that is already on the shelf is read_book, not story.
