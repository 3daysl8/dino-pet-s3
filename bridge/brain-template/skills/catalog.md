# Possible new skills

The nightly digest picks suggested fixes from this list (not sent in chats).
Add ideas as they come up. Format: name | what it adds | effort | privacy.

- Dictionary and spelling | spell words, word meanings for school | small | local word list
- Maths helper | sums, times tables, counting games, checked by code | small | local
- Unit converter | lengths, weights, temperatures | small | local
- TV and streaming guide | what's on tonight | medium | external listings site
- School timetable | what's on at school each day, library day, sport | small | local file a parent edits
- Stories | longer bedtime stories with a "story" mode and higher word limit | small | local
- Home Assistant | lights, heater, doorbell | large | local, needs a Home Assistant token
- Page reader | read a whole web page for longer answers (Playwright on the server) | medium | the page's site sees the request
- Reminders | "remind me at 5 to feed the cat" as a timer with a time of day | medium | local (toy timers)
- Shopping list | add/read items, shared with the family | medium | local file or a phone list app
- Music by request | play a song or artist | large | streaming service account
